# The Estate — Digital Brand Typography System

## CRITICAL: Font Role Mapping

| Role | Font Name | Weight | CSS Class | Usage |
|------|-----------|--------|-----------|-------|
| Display Serif | Bodoni Moda (opsz:18) | Regular | `.font-estate-display` | Hero headlines, taglines, pull-quotes, "Where Vitality is the Ultimate Luxury." |
| Body Serif | Bodoni Moda (opsz:9) | Regular | `.font-estate-body-serif` | Longer narrative copy, section intros, descriptive paragraphs |
| Primary Sans | Inter (Fann Grotesque sub) | Book/Regular/SemiBold | `.font-estate-sans` | Body text, captions, labels, navigation, UI elements |
| Display Sans | Josefin Sans (Darby Sans sub) | Light | `.font-estate-wordmark` | THE ESTATE wordmark ONLY, uppercase, tracking 0.3em |

## Master Wordmark — "THE ESTATE"
- Font: Josefin Sans Light (substitute for Darby Sans Poster Web Light)
- Treatment: Uppercase, very wide letter-spacing (tracking 200-400 units / 0.3em)
- Usage: Primary brand logo and major structural sign-offs

## Display & Editorial Headlines
- Font: Bodoni Moda 18pt Regular (and italic for taglines)
- Treatment: Large scale, generous line-height. Italic for signature tagline.
- Usage: Hero headlines, section openers, aspirational narrative statements

## Body Copy & Narrative Text
- Font: Bodoni Moda 9pt Regular (editorial) OR Inter Regular (informational)
- Treatment: Standard line-height (1.5-1.6x), 60-75 chars per line
- Usage: Descriptive paragraphs, biographies, partner descriptions

## UI, Labels & Navigation
- Font: Inter Regular or SemiBold (substitute for Fann Grotesque Pro)
- Treatment: Uppercase with generous tracking (100-200 units) for labels/nav; sentence case for buttons
- Usage: All interactive/structural interface elements, metadata, captions

## Color Palette
- Primary Dark: #000000 / #1A1A1A
- Primary Light: #FFFFFF
- Warm Neutral: #F5F0E8 (Ivory/Cream)
- Secondary Accent: #C4B5A0 (Warm Stone)
- Metallic Accent: #B8956B (Muted Gold) — SPARINGLY for highlights/separators only
- Utility Dark: #333333 (Charcoal)
